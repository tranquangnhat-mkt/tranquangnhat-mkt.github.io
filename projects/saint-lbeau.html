02 · Challenge

01

Multi-brand complexity

Four brands served different audiences and product categories, so one repeated content template would not work.

02

Consistency at scale

The team needed a steady publishing rhythm without losing each brand’s tone, visual cues and product priorities.

03

Content-to-commerce gap

Social content also had to improve product discovery and support marketplace campaigns on Shopee and Tiki.

04

Execution coordination

Creators, video production, e-commerce and CRM activities required clear briefs, timelines and follow-up.

03 · Strategic Approach

01

Brand-based system

Defined distinct content roles and visual directions for Iris Care, Pebble, tmrbae and ToningMe Tea.

02

Central planning

Used shared calendars, briefs and production plans to align campaigns and always-on content.

03

Product education

Turned benefits and use cases into social posts, expert-led films and preparation videos.

04

KOC collaboration

Developed creator and affiliate coordination to extend credible demonstrations and product reach.

05

Commerce loop

Connected social assets with marketplace storefronts, campaign support and performance follow-up.

08 · Key Learning

01

Build the brand system before scaling output.

A shared operating model works best when each brand still has a clear audience, visual code and content role.

02

Connect creator briefs to product proof.

Creator content becomes more useful when the benefit, demonstration and next action are defined from the start.

03

Treat social and marketplace as one journey.

Social creative, product pages and promotional campaigns should reinforce the same customer understanding.
